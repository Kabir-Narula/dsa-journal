# Weekly Sunday Reviews

Run `python scripts/tracker_cli.py stats` every Sunday (max 30 minutes) and fill in the weekly card below.

**Weekly Targets:** `>= 12` new problems | `>= 8` redos | `1` timed unseen problem | `1` recorded out-loud explanation

---

```markdown
- **Biggest Win of the Week:**

- **Timed Unseen Problem:** <Problem> (<Result>, <Time>)

- **Weakest Pattern / Most Frequent Mistake:**
- **Next Week's #1 Fix:**
```
